Who Was Who

Show this to the room once every team has shared their answer. Tap each card to reveal it.

Youssef

Case 1 · "The Smart One"

TAP
🔒 Fixed mindset

Youssef believes ability is a trait he already has, since everyone calls him "the smart one." He avoids anything that could disprove that: he does not retake the test, he dismisses the course, he deletes his own question so he does not look unsure, and he calls a low score "easy" to protect his image instead of engaging with the challenge.

Mariam

Case 2 · "The Quiet Worker"

TAP
🎭 Imposter syndrome

Mariam's results are genuinely strong: an early finish, a big improvement, and she helped classmates. But she cannot accept the evidence. She explains her success away ("only because I retook it"), rates herself far below reality, and privately considers quitting despite outperforming.

Nour

Case 3 · "The Planner in Progress"

TAP
🌱 Growth mindset

Nour gets frustrated first, which is normal, then does the real work: she diagnoses exactly where she went wrong, asks a specific question, retakes the test and improves, and rates herself honestly with a plan to reassess later. Struggle does not stop her, it becomes information.

Karim

Case 4 · "The Hype Man"

TAP
📣 Fake growth

Karim says all the right growth mindset lines out loud, but his actions do not match. He brute forces the test instead of learning from it, does not care about understanding a wrong answer once he has passed, rates himself "to be safe" instead of honestly, and plans to rush his project. It is growth mindset language without growth mindset behaviour.

Ask the room: what did each person say, and what did the evidence show?
That gap is the real lesson.
